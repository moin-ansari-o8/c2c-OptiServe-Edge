Sacred Heart College (Autonomous), Thevara

Sacred Heart College was established in 1967 by the Carmelites of Mary Immaculate (CMI) congregation. The college is located in Thevara, Kochi, Kerala, India.

The institution was granted autonomy by the University Grants Commission (UGC) in 2014. It is affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam.

Academic Programs:
The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs across three faculties:
- Faculty of Arts: English, Economics, History, Political Science
- Faculty of Science: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Statistics
- Faculty of Commerce: Commerce, Business Administration

Campus Facilities:
The campus spans approximately 25 acres with modern infrastructure:
- Central Library with over 80,000 volumes and digital resources
- Advanced computer laboratories with high-speed internet
- Science laboratories for physics, chemistry, and biology
- Sports grounds including cricket, football, basketball, and volleyball courts
- Auditorium with a seating capacity of 1500
- Cafeteria and student amenities centre

Research and Innovation:
Sacred Heart College has several research centres recognised by Mahatma Gandhi University. The college encourages interdisciplinary research and has collaborations with national and international institutions.

Cultural Activities:
The annual cultural festival is called Drishya. It features inter-collegiate competitions in music, dance, drama, and literary events. The festival typically runs for three days and attracts participants from colleges across Kerala.

Notable Alumni:
The college has produced many distinguished alumni in the fields of academics, civil services, business, and the arts.
